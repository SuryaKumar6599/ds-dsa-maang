Today’s Problem

Jump Game

Difficulty: Medium

Why this problem?

* Classic FAANG interview question.
* Teaches a very important Greedy Algorithm pattern.
* Easy to understand but tricky to prove correct.
* Frequently asked as a foundation for harder variants like:
    * Jump Game II
    * Minimum Number of Taps to Open to Water a Garden
    * Video Stitching

Skills Practiced

✅ Greedy thinking
✅ Maintaining farthest reachable position
✅ O(n) optimization
✅ Interview explanation skills

Expected Learning

Many candidates first think:

* Dynamic Programming
* Backtracking
* BFS

But the optimal solution is a simple O(n) Greedy approach.

Challenge

Try solving it in three stages:

1. Brute Force (recursive thinking)
2. DP/Memoization
3. Greedy O(n)

Then ask yourself:

“Why is checking the farthest reachable index sufficient?”

If you can explain that convincingly, you’ve learned the real interview lesson.
